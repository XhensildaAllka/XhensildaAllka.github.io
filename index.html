Background

Research-backed,
product-minded.

I hold a PhD (Cum Laude) in Data Science & Machine Learning from Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), where my research focused on improving the data quality of low-cost IoT air quality sensors using deep learning, anomaly detection, and probabilistic methods — resulting in 7 IEEE publications.

I currently work as a Research Engineer at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC), one of Europe's leading HPC research institutions, working on high-performance computing and numerical methods.

Beyond research, I build AI products end-to-end. I'm open to freelance collaborations in data science, ML engineering, and AI product development.

Low-Cost O₃ Sensor Calibration

ML and data science for low-cost sensor data quality improvement. Comparing MLR, SVR, KNN, FFNN and Gaussian Process models for calibrating electrochemical O₃ sensors against a reference monitoring station in Barcelona. Full pipeline with outlier removal, standardisation, and 5-fold cross-validation. Real dataset included.
