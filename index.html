About me

I'm a data scientist and researcher specialising in machine learning applied to real-world, imperfect data — specifically improving the reliability of low-cost IoT air quality sensors through deep learning, anomaly detection, and probabilistic methods.

I completed my PhD (Cum Laude) at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya and I'm currently a Research Engineer at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, working on high-performance computing — optimising algebraic multigrid solvers and contributing to a CUDA C multigrid library.

Beyond research, I'm genuinely curious about building things with AI. I recently designed and shipped a full AI personal assistant from scratch — combining multiple LLMs, voice interfaces, and Gmail integration.
